# Style Modes

Use this file to decide how the poster should look before generating directions.

## 1. Realistic Commercial Poster

Best for:

- revenge
- CEO / power stories
- urban romance
- family conflict
- suspense

Core traits:

- cinematic realism
- clear facial readability
- film-like lighting
- realistic skin, fabric, rain, glass, metal, space pressure
- strong commercial poster polish

Prompt language:

- 写实电影感
- 商业短剧海报底图
- 中近景人物关系清晰
- 主光、轮廓光、空间压迫

Avoid:

- anime line-art language
- exaggerated cartoon facial ratios
- game splash-art over-rendering that weakens the short-drama hook

## 2. Semi-Realistic Illustration

Best for:

- romance
- fantasy
- costume drama
- mood-heavy emotional posters

Core traits:

- realistic structure with painterly control
- cleaner skin and fabric than photo realism
- stronger color scripting
- facial identity still anchored to actor temperament when references exist

Prompt language:

- 半写实插画海报
- 影视级插画完成度
- 笔触控制
- 体积光与层次色块

Avoid:

- plastic AI skin
- flat low-detail coloring
- over-busy fantasy clutter

## 3. Anime / Cartoon Poster

Best for:

- light comedy
- youth romance
- fantasy with stylized characters
- family-friendly promotion

Core traits:

- readable silhouette first
- expression clarity stronger than texture realism
- controlled line style or cel-shading
- shape language must support relationship reading at thumbnail size

Prompt language:

- 动漫海报风格
- 赛璐璐上色 / 平涂卡通 / 漫画感
- 五官风格统一
- 发型与轮廓清晰
- 表情和站位体现关系

Avoid:

- forcing realistic pore or skin wording
- childish low-end clip-art look
- random mixed anime styles across characters

## 4. Stylized 3D Anime Poster

Best for:

- premium fantasy romance
- idol / youth / entertainment stories
- stylized high-gloss campaign visuals
- projects wanting "game cinematic but still readable as short-drama poster"

Core traits:

- anime facial design rendered with 3D depth
- polished materials, hair chunks, fabric folds, glossy lighting
- commercial key-art composition
- cinematic rim light and environment separation
- not fully realistic, not toy-like, not mobile-game trash aesthetic

Prompt language:

- 类 3D 动漫海报
- 高级三维动漫渲染
- 动漫角色比例
- 发丝块面清楚
- 面部干净、眼神明确
- 体积光 / 轮廓光 / 材质高光
- 商业 key visual 质感

Avoid:

- plastic doll look
- childish chibi proportions unless requested
- overcrowded VFX
- game login-screen UI feeling
- realistic actor skin wording if the user explicitly wants stylized anime identity

## 5. Cast Size Rules

### Single Character

- subject height around 60%-80%
- hook comes from gaze, posture, wardrobe, and prop

### Two Characters

- combined height around 65%-85%
- ideal for confrontation, coercion, romance tension, betrayal
- distance, facing direction, and light contrast must reveal the relationship

### Three Characters

- use triangle hierarchy
- one clear center of gravity
- ideal for emotional triangle, family pressure, two-against-one staging

### Four to Six Characters

- use pyramid or layered stagger composition
- only top 2-3 roles should receive full face priority
- supporting roles should reinforce conflict, not flatten it into a graduation photo

### More Than Six Characters

- simplify aggressively
- focus on core 3-5 roles
- others become atmosphere silhouettes or secondary layers
- do not promise equal detail on every face
